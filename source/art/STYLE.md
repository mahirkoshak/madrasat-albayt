# مدرسة البيت: art style guide ("Desert Daylight")

A playful, premium learning-game look for children aged 4–12, used in a Saudi home and read right-to-left.
Two families of art share one palette and one light direction, so they always look like one set.

## 1. Soft-3D (characters, mascot, avatars, medals, reward items, game pieces)
- Rounded, toy-like, clay/vinyl forms; no sharp edges.
- Big expressive eyes with two bright catchlights, small friendly mouth, soft rosy cheeks.
- Matte soft-touch materials, subtle subsurface glow. No glossy plastic shine, no metal glare.
- Light: warm key light from the upper left, a soft cool rim light from the right, gentle ambient occlusion on the object itself (no shadow cast on the background).
- Proportions: big head and small body for characters (about 1:1.2), compact chunky objects.
- Camera: straight-on or a slight 3/4 view, centred, the whole subject visible with about 10% margin.

## 2. Polished 2D (subject, grade, unit, lesson, game-card and topic illustrations, content pictures)
- Flat shapes with soft gradient shading (light top-left to shadow bottom-right), no outlines.
- Rounded corners and simple, readable silhouettes that still work at 64 px.
- One clear focal object plus at most 2–3 supporting shapes.
- Same palette and light direction as the soft-3D art.

## Rules for every image
- **No text, letters, numbers or logos inside the art**: the site adds all text itself, in Arabic and English.
- **Background:** a perfectly flat, uniform chroma green **#00FF00** with no shadow, gradient, floor or texture on it.
  **No shadow on the background**: no contact shadow, no floor. The site removes the green and adds its own soft shadow.
  Never use green #00FF00 inside the subject itself; use the palette green #58CC02 instead.
- Square 1024 × 1024 unless the asset list says otherwise.
- **People:** friendly cartoon children, diverse skin tones, hair and faces. Modest everyday Saudi-appropriate clothing:
  long sleeves or a t-shirt with long trousers. Girls may wear a hijab or have their hair visible.
  No real people and no likeness of existing cartoon characters or brands.
- **Original designs only:** nothing resembling a known character, mascot, toy or brand.
- Child-safe and warm. Nothing scary: germs and illnesses are cute and simple, never gross.
- Right-to-left aware: when a character points or walks toward "next", it faces **left**.

## Mascot: "كوكو" the koala
- A small, round, soft-3D koala child: dove-grey fur with a cream belly and inner ears, big fluffy ears,
  a large oval dark nose, big warm brown eyes, and rosy cheeks.
- Wears a small mint-teal backpack (#2BBBAD) with a gold star badge. This is its signature item, in every pose.
- Personality: curious, encouraging, gently funny, never mocking.
